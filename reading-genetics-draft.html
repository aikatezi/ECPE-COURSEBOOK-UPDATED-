Before You Read

A

Many of our personality traits and physical characteristics are hereditary, that is, they are passed down from one generation to another through our DNA. What traits do you think you have inherited from your parents? Who do you resemble most in your family?

Show vocabulary

hereditary — κληρονομικός
resemble — μοιάζω

B

Match the words in color in sentences 1-8 to their meanings (a-h).

  1. As the number of tigers in the wild decreases, there is less variation in the gene pool.
  2. Hemophilia is caused by a genetic defect.
  3. Pollutants can cause infertility in animals.
  4. Evidence provided by forensic science helped to identify the murderer.
  5. Freedom of speech and religion are examples of civil liberties.
  6. The prime suspect is male and blond, and he has a scar on his left cheek.
  7. Having already stolen five cars, that boy is now classified as a habitual offender.
  8. The perpetrator of last week’s robbery has been arrested.
