Model Essay

Risk-Taking Behavior

Most young people engage in some form of risky behavior in their teens. According to research, the incidence of risk-taking rises sharply up to the age of 18, and then begins to decrease. However, although such behavior presents dangers, it can also be beneficial for young people to be willing to take risks.

The major problem with risky behavior is the physical danger it poses. Safety precautions tend to be ignored by teens either because they underestimate the risks involved in their actions or because they may feel invincible, believing that nothing can harm them. For example, in a recent poll, 68% of teenagers admitted to having put their lives in jeopardy by riding a motorcycle or scooter without a helmet. Some teens adopt behaviors that they know to be harmful in the long term because they are only concerned with the present moment. For example, many young people take up smoking as a result of peer pressure and to conform to group behaviors, ignoring the future health risk.

On the other hand, the fact that adolescents are more inclined to take risks is essential to their development. As one psychologist put it, "Teenagers need to be prepared to take some risks to help them develop independence, new skills, and a sense of identity." Young people cannot benefit from challenging new activities, such as performing in public, without stepping out of their comfort zone and finding out what they are capable of. This involves an element of risk-taking. In addition, the courage to take chances can lead to positive changes in society. A good example of this is teen activist Greta Thunberg, who risked expulsion from school in order to demonstrate against climate change.

To conclude, adolescence is a time when risk-taking can lead young people into danger, but I do not believe they should always be encouraged to avoid risks altogether. Schools and parents should educate young people about unnecessary danger, while encouraging them to take healthy risks that may benefit them in the long term.

ECPE Essay Guide

How to Write a Strong Essay

1. Understand the Task

Στο ECPE essay δεν γράφουμε γενικά ό,τι ξέρουμε για το θέμα. Πρώτα εντοπίζουμε το general statement, μετά την ερώτηση και μετά την οδηγία: πρέπει να address the topic, explain your opinion και include at least one piece of information given.

2. Use the Given Information

Τα δεδομένα δεν τα αντιγράφουμε τυχαία. Τα χρησιμοποιούμε ως support. Αν είναι statistic, το ενώνουμε με point. Αν είναι quote, το χρησιμοποιούμε για να στηρίξουμε άποψη. Πάντα αναφέρουμε την πηγή.

3. Paragraph Plan

  • Paragraph 1: introduce the topic and give your general opinion.
  • Paragraph 2: first main side of the issue, with explanation and example/source.
  • Paragraph 3: second main side of the issue, with explanation and example/source.
  • Paragraph 4: conclusion, final opinion and recommendation.

4. Length and Style

Δεν υπάρχει αυστηρό ανώτατο όριο λέξεων, αλλά ένας ασφαλής στόχος είναι περίπου 250-300 words. Το ύφος πρέπει να είναι formal: αποφεύγουμε short forms όπως don't, can't, it's.

5. Avoid Personal Style

Καλό είναι να αποφεύγουμε υπερβολική χρήση πρώτου προσώπου, ειδικά I και we. Αντί για We consider that..., προτιμάμε πιο academic διατύπωση: It can be argued that... ή This issue should be considered carefully.

6. Use Passive Voice

Η παθητική φωνή βοηθάει να ακούγεται το essay πιο formal και objective. Για παράδειγμα: Teenagers should be educated about online risks αντί για We should educate teenagers about online risks.
