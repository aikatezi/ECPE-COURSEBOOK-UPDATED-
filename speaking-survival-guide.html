Language bank

Useful Vocabulary & Expressions

Οι εκφράσεις της φωτογραφίας σου οργανωμένες ανά λειτουργία, ώστε ο μαθητής να βρίσκει γρήγορα τι χρειάζεται σε κάθε stage.

Presenting & summarizing

  • To begin with, of the two options I was given, the first one is ...
  • Of the two ... I have here, the first one is ...
  • According to my notes, the second option ...
  • As far as I can see, the second option ...
  • In addition, / What's more, / Moreover, ...
  • What is also mentioned here is that ...
  • I would also like to add that ...
  • However, / Nevertheless, / On the downside, ...
  • A disadvantage concerning this option is ...
  • A negative aspect is the fact that ...
  • Something negative about ... though, is ...
  • Nevertheless, a negative point may be that ...

Comparing, contrasting & reaching a consensus

  • The first idea has a lot in its favor, as ...
  • It would seem to me that ... is the better choice ...
  • Of the two, I think that ... is more suitable because ...
  • ... would be an excellent choice because ...
  • I'd say that ... is the more appropriate choice.
  • What do you have to say about ...?
  • What do you think? Do you agree?
  • I wouldn't recommend ... since ...
  • On the other hand, ... would be a problem as ...
  • Well, in my opinion ... is more important ... although I do see your point about ...
  • I can't say I agree with you because we should also bear in mind that ...
  • I agree with you up to a point, but ...
  • Yes, I see what you mean, but shouldn't we also consider that ...?
  • Yes, you're probably right about that.
  • That's a very good point, actually.
  • Yes, I suppose you're right.
  • I'll go along with your choice, then.

Recommending an option to your partner

  • Well, if I were you, I would choose ...
  • Of the two choices you have presented, I feel that the best one would be ...
  • Although ... sounds like a good idea, I would go for ...
  • I would have to suggest the first option ...

Stating your chosen option

  • I would have to select the first option ...
  • I think that ... is really worth considering, so I'll go with that.
  • I'm finding it difficult to choose between them, but I've decided on ...
  • Although both options seem appropriate, I'm choosing ...

Presenting & convincing

  • Let me start by saying that all four ... seem to be great choices, but we thought ...
  • After a lot of thought, we have decided on ...
  • My partner and I have decided that ... would be the right choice as ...
  • We are convinced that ... is the ideal option, seeing that ...
  • First and foremost, I would like to point out that ...
  • A further point in favor of this option is ...
  • Now, my partner will continue our presentation by telling you about ...
  • Also, we cannot disregard the fact that ...
  • Another reason we prefer this option is ...
  • For these reasons, we believe that this option is a good fit.

Justifying your choice

  • What convinced us that ... was more suitable was ...
  • We ended up choosing ... mainly because ...
  • What led us to this choice was ...
  • We both agree that priority should be given to ...
  • The deciding factor for us was that ...

Defending your choice

  • Despite the obvious benefits of ..., we chose ...
  • We seriously considered ..., but decided that ...
  • Although ... seemed like a good choice, on second thought it wasn't, because ...
  • I would certainly agree with that, but we focused on ...
  • Yes, you are definitely right, but we don't find it a serious problem.

Asking for clarification

  • Could you please repeat/rephrase that?
  • When you say ..., do you mean ...?
  • What do you mean by ...?
  • If I understood correctly, you want to say that ...
  • I don't think I got your point. Do you mean that ...?
  • Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Did you say that ...?
  • I'm not sure I understood what you meant.
  • Are you trying to say that ...?
Exam map

Πώς λειτουργεί το ECPE Speaking

Το Revised ECPE Speaking είναι περίπου 30 λεπτά, γίνεται με 2 ή 3 υποψηφίους και 2 examiners και αποτελείται από 5 στάδια που σταδιακά γίνονται πιο απαιτητικά.

1

Introductions

Συστήνεστε και απαντάτε σε σύντομες προσωπικές ερωτήσεις. Στόχος: φυσική επικοινωνία και ολοκληρωμένες απαντήσεις, όχι μονολεκτικά.

2

Summarize & Recommend

Κάθε υποψήφιος έχει διαφορετικές επιλογές. Παρουσιάζεις/παραφράζεις τις πληροφορίες της κάρτας σου, ζυγίζεις θετικά και αρνητικά και προτείνεις επιλογή στον partner.

3

Negotiate

Συζητάτε τις επιλογές που προέκυψαν, συγκρίνετε, συμφωνείτε ή διαφωνείτε ευγενικά και προσπαθείτε να καταλήξετε σε κοινή απόφαση.

4

Present

Παρουσιάζετε την κοινή απόφαση/θέση στους examiners. Οργανώστε την παρουσίαση, μοιράστε τον λόγο και δώστε σαφείς λόγους υπέρ της επιλογής.

5

Justify & Defend

Οι examiners κάνουν follow-up ερωτήσεις ή αμφισβητούν σημεία της επιλογής. Υπερασπίζεστε τη θέση σας με αιτιολόγηση, διευκρινίσεις και κατάλληλη αλληλεπίδραση.

Τι αξιολογείται στην πράξη: δεν αρκεί να έχεις «σωστή» επιλογή. Χρειάζεται να δείξεις C2-level interaction: να ακούς τον partner, να ανταποκρίνεσαι σε όσα λέει, να παραφράζεις αντί να διαβάζεις, να αναπτύσσεις επιχειρήματα και να κρατάς τη συζήτηση ζωντανή.
STAGE 1 · INTRODUCTIONS

Warm-up Questions

Practice answering naturally, adding reasons and examples. Use the sample questions from the source book below.

Let's get talking!

  1. What's your name? How old are you?
  2. Tell me something about your family. What do your parents do?
  3. Do you have any brothers or sisters?
  4. Do you have any pets? Would you like to have any?
  5. Where are you from?
  6. How long have you lived in your town?
  7. What is your town like?
  8. What is there for young people to do in your town?
  9. What could a visitor see or do in your hometown?
  10. How do you get along with the other members of your family?
  11. What's your favorite school subject? Which career really interests you?
  12. What would you like to see improved in your hometown?
  13. What kinds of hobbies or leisure activities are you interested in?
  14. How do you usually spend your free time?
  15. Do you like surfing the Net? How much time do you usually spend online?
  16. Have you ever been abroad?
  17. Why do you learn English?
  18. What do you need the certificate of proficiency for?
